## Architecture decision
Generate hermes/package from canonical source discovery. Registered documents share a parent;
their referenced content remains beneath it so native linked-file serving needs no traversal.
Keep the manifest, entrypoint, adapted contracts, required transitive references/scripts,
licenses, NOTICE and source/destination provenance in the candidate. Development generators and
their build-only dependencies stay outside it. Preserve public names, anchors and meaningful
frontmatter, with zero additional tools/hooks/capabilities. Agent metadata is not host enforcement.
Inclusions and exclusions follow supported product dependencies, never scanner findings.
Do not fetch omitted content later or mask security-relevant strings. Fail on unresolved required
references, ambiguous paths, escapes, collisions or unsupported operational syntax.

Alternative: distribute the complete repository and seek a narrowly justified upstream scanner
fix. That keeps a larger surface and depends on separate upstream review, so it is not this sprint.
